# Parkrift

Park, drift and race through a living city, on your own or head to head with a friend.

Parkrift is a car game for iPhone, played in landscape. It starts with tight parking puzzles and opens up into a full city with traffic, traffic lights, pedestrians and police. Every mode ends the same way: a clean result screen with your stars, your time and the coins you earned.

## Modes

- **Parking:** 50 levels, from a simple straight bay to tight parallel parks, angled bays and back-in garages. Later levels add time limits, crash limits, rain and snow, and patrol cars that drive through the lot. Park inside the lines, straight and still, to finish.
- **City:** five rounds in an open city. Follow the route on the mini map through live traffic and park in the marked spot before the clock runs out.
- **Race:** three rounds across the city to a chequered finish line. Crashes cost time, so the cleanest fast run wins.
- **Online:** play any of these against a friend. Share a four-letter room code or use quick match. Matches are best of three rounds, and the first to finish takes the round.

## What makes it different

- **Precision meter:** after every park, the bay is measured in centimetres. Gaps to the lines and the angle of the car give you a precision score and a grade from C to S.
- **A city that drives:** traffic keeps its lane, gives way at crossings, stops at red lights and honks when you block it.
- **Police and speed cameras:** speed past a camera or crash into someone and a police car comes after you. Escape it or get pulled over.
- **Drift and nitro:** drift through corners to fill your nitro, then use it for a burst of speed.
- **Garage:** unlock new cars with the coins you earn, paint them and upgrade them.
- **Daily tasks:** fresh goals every day, with rewards you collect by hand.
- **Weather and night:** rain, snow and night drives with headlights.
